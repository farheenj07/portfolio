Global EEO and DEI policy statement

Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) and Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) policies is aimed to create an inclusive, respectful workplace, strictly prohibiting discrimination based on race, gender, age, disability, sexual orientation, and other protected characteristics. Work practice is focused on increasing representation, accessibility, and leveraging Employee Resource Groups.


Key Components of policies:

Equal Pay & Opportunities: Commitment to providing fair, equitable compensation for work of equal value.

Merit-Based Employment: Recruitment, promotion, and development decisions are made based on skills and performance, eliminating bias.

Inclusive Culture & Belonging: Initiatives focused on creating environments where diverse perspectives are welcomed and employees feel valued.

Accessibility & Accommodation: Providing reasonable adjustments for employees with disabilities and supporting diverse needs through effective skill evaluation and understanding.

Zero Tolerance for Discrimination & Harassment: Companies enforce strict prohibitions against bullying, harassment, and discrimination of any kind, ensuring a respectful workplace.
